Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Articles 6 and 8

1. **Scope:** This Policy applies to competitions organized or supported in Speedcubing Türkiye's name.
2. **WCA rules:** These competitions follow the WCA's rules (including the WCA Competition Requirements Policy), the Bylaws, the Motions and the Policies.
3. **Organizing team:** The organizing team includes at least one Full Member.
4. **Budget:** Before registration opens, the organizer prepares a competition budget, which the Treasurer and the Executive Director approve. If either of them has their own costs in the budget, another Board member with no costs in the budget approves in their place. If a competition makes a loss, Speedcubing Türkiye covers it. Any surplus stays with Speedcubing Türkiye and is used for the benefit of the community.
5. **Fees:** Registration fees and refund terms are announced clearly before registration opens. Fees cover the competition's costs without making it hard to take part.
6. **Refunds:** The organizers set the refund rate and the refund deadline.
7. **Payment:** How registration fees are paid is announced on the competition's WCA page. Cash taken on the day of the competition is paid into the account under Motion 06.2026.1 Finances.
8. **Reductions:** The Board may offer fee reductions or waivers for people who would otherwise struggle to afford to take part. Requests are kept confidential.
9. **Income and expense summary:** Within 15 days after the competition, the organizer gives the Treasurer an income and expense summary with receipts.
10. **Sponsors:** Competition sponsors follow the Sponsorship Policy.
