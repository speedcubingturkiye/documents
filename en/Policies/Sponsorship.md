Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Articles 3 and 8

1. **WCA sponsors:** No sponsorship is accepted that conflicts with the WCA's sponsors (WCA Motion 06.2025.1, section 3.2). In case of doubt, the Executive Director asks the WCA.
2. **Approval:** The Board approves sponsorship agreements made on behalf of Speedcubing Türkiye.
3. **Written agreement:** What the sponsor gives (money, prizes, products) and what it gets in return (such as logos, announcements or a stand) is recorded in writing.
4. **Equal opportunity:** For major events such as the Turkish Championship, an open call for sponsors may be made; offers are assessed against the same criteria.
5. **Seeking sponsors:** Speedcubing Türkiye, its Officers and organizers may look for sponsors in writing or verbally, and may use the "Sponsorship packs" that Speedcubing Türkiye provides for this. Agreements are subject to the Board's approval.
6. **Compliance with WCA rules:** Sponsorship may not affect running the competition in line with WCA rules. Sponsor promotion follows the WCA's rules.
7. **Conflicts of interest:** An Officer connected to a sponsor (as disclosed under the Ethics Policy) takes no part in decisions about that sponsorship.
8. **Transparency:** Sponsorship income is shown in the competition's income and expense summary and in the annual financial report.
9. **Ending a sponsorship:** If a sponsorship comes to conflict with this Policy or the WCA's rules, the Board may end it.
