Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Article 8

1. **Shared property:** Speedcubing Türkiye's equipment belongs to the organization as a whole (Bylaws Article 8).
2. **Equipment list:** The Executive Director keeps the equipment list (what it is, when it was acquired, where it is and who holds it) and presents it to the General Assembly each year.
3. **Lending:** Equipment may be lent to organizers of WCA competitions in Türkiye. Speedcubing Türkiye's own competitions come first.
4. **Lending terms:** Handover and return are recorded in writing. The borrower returns the equipment in the condition it was received and reports any loss or damage straight away. The Board decides how loss or damage is covered.
5. **Stocktake:** The Board checks and counts the equipment at least once a year.
6. **Buying:** New equipment is bought within the budget and under the approval rule in Bylaws Article 8. Where possible, WCA equipment support is applied for.
