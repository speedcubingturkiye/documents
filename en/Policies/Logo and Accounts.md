Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Articles 6 and 8

1. **Using the logo:** Speedcubing Türkiye's name and logo are used at events organized or supported in its name and in official communication. Any other use needs the Board's written permission. Detailed rules of use are in the guide published at speedcubing.org.
2. **Form of the logo:** The form of the logo is set out in the rules of the guide published at speedcubingturkiye.org.
3. **Commercial use:** The name and logo may not be used in a way that suggests Speedcubing Türkiye endorses a product or company, except under sponsorship agreements the Board has approved.
4. **Accounts:** The domain name (speedcubingturkiye.org), email, social media and other online accounts are opened for Speedcubing Türkiye and belong to the organization. While it has no legal personality, the domain is registered in the name of a Board member, who holds it in trust for Speedcubing Türkiye. When someone with access to Speedcubing Türkiye's accounts leaves or is removed from their role, their access to those accounts is removed.
5. **Access:** At least two Officers have access to every account. Passwords are kept in a shared password manager and are not tied to personal email addresses.
6. **List of accounts:** The Secretary keeps the list of accounts (the account, who is responsible and who has access).
7. **Handover:** When someone's role ends, their access is removed and passwords are changed within 15 days.
8. **Domain name:** The domain's renewal date is tracked; it is renewed at least 60 days before it expires.
9. **Posts:** The Communications team is responsible for posts from official accounts. Photos of children are shared under the Safeguarding Policy.
