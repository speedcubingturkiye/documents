Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Articles 4 and 6

1. **The law:** Speedcubing Türkiye processes personal data in line with Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The privacy notice is published on the website.
2. **Data collected:** Only the data needed for membership and announcements is collected. Data is never sold and is not shared with third parties without the person's consent or a legal obligation.
3. **Competition registration:** Competition registrations go through the WCA's own systems and are subject to the WCA's privacy rules.
4. **Access:** Only Officers who need it for their role can access the membership lists.
5. **Deletion:** When membership ends, the person's data is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires keeping it. Anyone may ask for their data to be deleted.
6. **Requests:** Requests about personal data are sent to info@speedcubingturkiye.org and answered within 30 days at most.
7. **Photos and videos:** Photos and videos taken at events are used according to the consent given at registration. Photos and videos of children are used only with that consent. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
8. **Keeping files:** Disciplinary and complaint files are kept for 5 years after the decision and then deleted.
9. **Responsibility:** The Secretary is responsible for personal data.
