Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Articles 2 and 6

1. **Frequency:** The Turkish Championship is held at most once a year.
2. **Organizing and open call:** The Board may organize the championship itself or publish an open call for organizers. The Board decides when to publish the call. The call states the criteria, the deadline and the information required.
3. **Bids:** A bid gives the city and venue, the proposed date, the organizing team (including at least one Full Member), the budget and sponsorship plan, and capacity and travel information.
4. **Criteria:** The venue's suitability and capacity, travel and accommodation, the organizing team's experience, a realistic budget, and holding the championship in different cities over time.
5. **Selection:** Bids are assessed by a panel made up of the Board and the WCA Delegates in Türkiye. Anyone connected to a bid stays out of assessing it. The panel publishes its decision with reasons.
6. **No bids:** If no suitable bid is received, the Board organizes the championship itself or chooses an organizing team by agreement.
7. **Title:** Who is eligible for the title of Turkish Champion is determined by the WCA's rules.
