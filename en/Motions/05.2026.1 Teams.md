Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Article 6

1. **Teams:** The Board sets up and closes teams to carry out the work. Where no team has been set up for a task, the Board is responsible for it.
2. **Remit:** Each team works under a written remit from the Board, which states what the team does and what it may decide.
3. **Team lead:** Each team has a lead. The Board appoints team leads for 1 year; a lead may be reappointed. A team lead does not have to be a Board member.
4. **Team members:** The Board chooses the team members. Any Member may join a team; anyone under 18 joins with parental consent.
5. **Reporting:** Before each Board meeting, team leads give the Executive Director a short report.
6. **Money:** Teams may not make payments on their own; payments are approved under Bylaws Article 8.
7. **Ethics:** Team leads are Officers and sign the Ethics Policy.
8. **Removal:** The Board may remove a team lead. If a team lead leaves, the Board appoints a new one within 30 days.
