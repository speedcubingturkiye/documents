Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Article 4

1. **Membership form:** The form asks for first name, surname, email address, date of birth (for the age check) and city. The form can be filled in by hand or with a WCA account. The WCA ID is added, verified by the WCA, when the Member signs up with their WCA account or links it later.
2. **Parental consent:** Anyone under 18 submits the form by ticking the box “By submitting this form, I declare that I have the consent of my parent or guardian.”
3. **Members' benefits:** Members receive announcements, may make suggestions and may observe the General Assembly. The Board may later give Members benefits such as discounts.
4. **Helping run an event:** Being on an event's organizing team, working at it as a judge, scrambler, runner, data entry volunteer or check-in volunteer, or being a member of a team counts as helping run it. The Board decides whether a job that is not on this list counts.
5. **Applying for Full Membership:** A Member who has helped run at least one Speedcubing Türkiye event in the last 12 months applies. The Board decides within 30 days and gives its reasons.
6. **Invitation:** The Board may invite a Member who has contributed to become a Full Member. The invitee becomes a Full Member on accepting.
7. **Appeal:** A refused applicant may appeal to the Audit Board within 15 days of being notified. Its decision is final.
8. **Losing voting rights:** A person who helps run no event for 24 months and does not answer a reminder within 30 days loses their voting rights. They remain a Member and may apply again.
9. **Leaving:** A person who wants to leave does so on the My account page of the website or says so by email.
10. **Lists:** The Secretary keeps the lists of Members and Full Members. The names of Full Members are visible to all Full Members.
