Adopted: 10 October 2026 · Basis: Bylaws Article 3

1. **Responsibility:** The Executive Director handles relations and official correspondence with the WCA. A copy of all official correspondence is added to the records kept by the Secretary.
2. **Sending documents:** The Executive Director sends the current Bylaws and Motions to the WCA electronically. After each change, the new version is sent within 30 days. The Bylaws are sent in Turkish and English.
3. **Annual report:** Every year, within 3 months of the end of the financial year, Speedcubing Türkiye gives the WCA an activity report. Besides anything the WCA asks for, it covers at least: the competitions and events held, membership numbers, a financial summary for the year and changes on the Boards. The Executive Director prepares the report and the Board approves it.
4. **List of board members:** The members of the Board and of the Audit Board are reported to the WCA; after any change, the updated list is sent within 30 days.
5. **Authorized persons and organizations:** A list is kept of the persons and organizations authorized to organize competitions on behalf of Speedcubing Türkiye, and it is sent to the WCA.
6. **Continental championships:** If the organization team of a continental championship in Europe asks for help (Bylaws Article 3), the Board decides what form the help takes (such as volunteers, equipment or communication) and appoints someone responsible.
7. **Reporting to the WCA:** Matters within the WCA's remit (for example, a conduct problem at a WCA competition) are reported to the relevant WCA body. The Audit Board may do this without waiting for the Board's approval (Bylaws Article 7).
8. **Conflicts:** If a Motion or Policy is found to conflict with a WCA rule, that provision does not apply and the Board corrects the text within 30 days.
9. **WCA support:** The Executive Director applies for WCA support programs (such as support for equipment, setting up and legal status); support received is shown separately in the annual financial report.
